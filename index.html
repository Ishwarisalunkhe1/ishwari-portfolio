Featured project In development

QUEIFY — Self-Checkout Queue Portal

A smoother self-checkout and queue experience

QUEIFY is a web application project focused on improving the self-checkout and queue experience. It is being developed with React, Vite, Tailwind CSS, React Router, and Supabase, with separate user roles for customers, business owners, and administrators.

What I'm building

  • Role-based views for customers, business owners, and administrators.
  • A simpler self-checkout flow with clearer queue status.
  • A React front end connected to a Supabase backend.
